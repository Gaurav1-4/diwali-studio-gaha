## Features & Implementation
- 10 authentic Indian pyrotechnics (Rocket, Anar, Starburst, Roman Candle, Phooljhadi, Grand Finale, Ganga Jamuna, Chakri, Sutli Bomb, Bullet Bomb).
- WebGL2 instanced spark shader with tapered exposure and thermal halos.
- Offloaded MediaPipe hand tracking to a dedicated Web Worker at 60 FPS.
- Adaptive performance engine dynamically scaling particle budgets from 1,600 to 7,500 particles.
- Real-time procedural audio synthesis and in-browser reel recording.
