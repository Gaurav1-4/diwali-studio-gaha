A little guidance.

Every sign uses one hand except the finale, which uses both open palms. Tap different spots to add more crackers, or change signs to layer effects. Fountains, sparklers and launchers follow their held signs. All ten crackers are visible on phones. Either hand works.

Enable the camera to use hand gestures.

Point up for Rocket, make a V for Roman candle, a fist for Anar, and pinch thumb to index for Phooljhadi. An L makes Starburst; both open palms start the finale. Hold one open palm to create more Chakris, or three fingers to stack Sutli bombs. Thumbs up fires Bullet. Ganga Jamuna uses index and little finger.
